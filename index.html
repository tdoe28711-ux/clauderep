What I'm working on

In progress
Self-directed research — 2026

What happens to athletes after the game ends

Professional athletes spend a career inside a structure that schedules their days, defines their identity, and disappears the moment they stop playing. I'm researching that transition directly with retired players to find out where the existing support actually breaks down.

This one didn't come from a brief. I grew up in this world, and it's a problem I kept hearing about and couldn't find anyone designing for. The design direction will come from what the research says, not the other way around.

Problem space defined

Narrowed to post-professional transition after mapping the wider territory.

Research instrument built

A written study designed for a hard-to-reach group, with open-ended prompts and a support-rating scale.

Fieldwork

Collecting responses from retired professional players.

Synthesis & design

Themes first, problem statement second, screens last.
